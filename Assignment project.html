Christiano Ronaldo

1985-Current

Greatest of All Time

Cristiano Ronaldo, often regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time, is a Portuguese professional footballer known for his exceptional skill, athleticism, and goal-scoring prowess. Born on February 5, 1985, in Madeira, Portugal, Ronaldo's illustrious career has seen him play for top clubs, including Sporting Lisbon, Manchester United, Real Madrid, and Juventus. Renowned for his incredible work ethic and dedication to his craft, Ronaldo's success is underpinned by his remarkable goal-scoring records and numerous individual awards, including multiple FIFA Ballon d'Or titles. His versatility, speed, and technical ability make him a force on the field, and his charismatic personality has earned him a massive global fanbase. Beyond football, Ronaldo is also recognized for his philanthropy, contributing to various charitable causes. His impact on the sport and his ability to consistently perform at the highest level have solidified his status as a football icon.

Life Achievement matches

  • 2003-2009: Early Career and Manchester United
  • 2003: Ronaldo joins Manchester United from Sporting Lisbon.
  • 2008: Wins his first FIFA World Player of the Year award.
  • 2008: Helps Manchester United win the Premier League and the UEFA Champions League.
  • 2009-2018: Real Madrid Dominance
  • 2009: Transfers to Real Madrid for a then-world record fee.
  • 2013: Secures the FIFA Ballon d'Or for the first time with Real Madrid.
  • 2014, 2015, 2017: Wins consecutive FIFA Ballon d'Or awards.
  • 2016: Helps Portugal win the UEFA European Championship.
  • 2017, 2018: Secures back-to-back UEFA Champions League titles with Real Madrid.
  • 2018-Present: Juventus and Continued Success
  • 2018: Transfers to Juventus in Serie A.
  • 2019: Becomes the first player to score 700 career goals.
  • 2020: Wins Serie A title with Juventus.
  • 2021: Becomes the all-time leading goal scorer in the UEFA Champions League.
  • 2021: Surpasses 800 career goals.
  • 2021: Returns to Manchester United.
  • 2022: Wins his fifth FIFA Ballon d'Or, tying Lionel Messi for the most wins.
